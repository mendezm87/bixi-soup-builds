# There's a Bixi in My Soup: playtest builds

**A co-op cooking game for 2 to 15 friends, with a saboteur in the kitchen.**

You're a crew of felt fruit and veg running a busy little restaurant: take orders, cook, plate up
and get the food out before the customers lose patience. But one of you is secretly a **Bixi**, a
kitchen gremlin in disguise, quietly poisoning the food. When a customer turns green and faints,
ring the bell twice for a **kitchen meeting**, argue over the evidence, and vote someone into the
walk-in freezer. Get it right and the kitchen is safe. Get it wrong and the Bixi keeps cooking with you.

- **Cook for real:** grill, fry, chop and plate. Only fully cooked food is safe, because heat cooks the
  poison out, so the Bixi strikes after it's cooked or talks someone into serving it rare.
- **Taste-test anything:** hold F on a dish to check it. From across the kitchen it looks exactly like
  someone poisoning it.
- **Talk it out:** proximity voice chat. Whisper by the fryer, shout across the kitchen, and everyone
  hears everyone in a meeting.
- **Secret roles:** Health Inspector, Sous Chef, Busboy, Bouncer, the Intern (who wants to be frozen),
  the Food Critic and the Secret Shopper on the crew's side. Rat, Arsonist and Mimic Bixis, and the
  Bixi's Accomplice, on the other.
- **Three kitchens:** The Good Egg Diner, Mama Mozza's Pizzeria, and the Rolling Tides Sushi Bar with
  its conveyor belts.
- **A week of nights:** make the nightly quota, spend tips on upgrades between nights, and survive
  Karens, rushes, blackouts and whatever twist each night brings.

It's an early playtest build made by two friends, so expect rough edges.

## Download and play

### Windows
**Download:** https://github.com/mendezm87/bixi-soup-builds/releases/latest/download/BixiSoup-playtest.zip

1. Unzip it anywhere (don't run it from inside the zip). Keep `BixiSoup.pck` next to `BixiSoup.exe`.
2. Double-click `BixiSoup.exe`.
3. Windows may warn that the game isn't recognised: it's a new, unsigned test build. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Mac
**Download:** https://github.com/mendezm87/bixi-soup-builds/releases/latest/download/BixiSoup-mac.zip

For Apple Silicon (M1 and newer) and Intel Macs, macOS 11 or newer. The Mac version is brand new, so tell us (F8) how it goes.

1. Unzip it and drag **There's a Bixi in My Soup** to your Applications folder.
2. The first time, **right-click** (or Control-click) the app, choose **Open**, then **Open** again. After that, open it normally.
3. If macOS says it "can't be opened" or "is damaged": go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. Still stuck? In Terminal, run
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/There's a Bixi in My Soup.app"` and open it again.
4. Allow the microphone when it asks: that's for voice chat.

### Both
Have Steam running to play with friends (Mac and Windows players should be able to play together). New to it? Start with **Training Shift** on the main menu.

What changed in each build is on the [release page](https://github.com/mendezm87/bixi-soup-builds/releases/latest).

## Found a bug?

Press **F8** in the game, say what happened, and press Send. It arrives here as an issue, with a
screenshot. Nothing that gives away your role is included.

This repo only holds the built game; the source is private.
